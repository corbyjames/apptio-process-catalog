## Strategy & Annual Planning

| Process | Cadence | Fiscal months | Catalog refs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy refresh & OKR setting | Annual | FM12, FM1 | 01.1.1, 01.2.1 |
| Annual budget: top-down targets | Annual | FM9 | 05.1.2, 02.4.2 |

**Strategy refresh & OKR setting** — Refresh strategic themes and set enterprise/portfolio OKRs for the new fiscal year.

**Annual budget: top-down targets** — Finance sets target spend and headcount envelopes and distributes them to budget owners (targets ride the data highway to the portfolio).

**Annual budget: bottom-up entry** — Budget owners build OpEx/CapEx line items — labor, contracts, assets, investments.

**Annual budget: review & iterate** — Submit/review/approve/return cycles reconcile top-down targets with bottom-up plans.

**Annual budget: finalize budget of record** — Lock the approved plan as budget of record with a snapshot for variance baselines.

**Participatory budgeting / lean budget adjustment** — Adjust value-stream budgets and guardrails at the mid-year and year-end boundaries.
